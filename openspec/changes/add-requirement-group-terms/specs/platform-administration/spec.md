# platform-administration

Requirement headings gain a Group Term. Administrator reach, the queue, bans and account deletion are
unchanged in wording and in behaviour. `Guard Administration` is shared with `web-client`, which gates
the same surface in the browser.

## RENAMED Requirements

- FROM: `### Requirement: An Administrator reaches every Note`
- TO: `### Requirement: Administer Notes - An Administrator reaches every Note`

- FROM: `### Requirement: The Moderation Queue lists work awaiting a decision`
- TO: `### Requirement: List Moderation Queue - The Moderation Queue lists work awaiting a decision`

- FROM: `### Requirement: An Administrator bans and unbans Accounts`
- TO: `### Requirement: Ban Account - An Administrator bans and unbans Accounts`

- FROM: `### Requirement: An Administrator deletes Accounts`
- TO: `### Requirement: Delete Account - An Administrator deletes Accounts`

- FROM: `### Requirement: Administration is closed to everyone else`
- TO: `### Requirement: Guard Administration - Administration is closed to everyone else`
