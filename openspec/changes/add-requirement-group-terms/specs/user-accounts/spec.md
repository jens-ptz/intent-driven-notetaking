# user-accounts

Requirement headings gain a Group Term. Registration, profile management, self-deletion and identity
release are unchanged in wording and in behaviour.

## RENAMED Requirements

- FROM: `### Requirement: Visitors register their own Account`
- TO: `### Requirement: Register Account - Visitors register their own Account`

- FROM: `### Requirement: Account holders manage their own profile`
- TO: `### Requirement: Manage Profile - Account holders manage their own profile`

- FROM: `### Requirement: Self-deletion cascades to the holder's Notes`
- TO: `### Requirement: Delete Own Account - Self-deletion cascades to the holder's Notes`

- FROM: `### Requirement: A deleted identity is released for reuse`
- TO: `### Requirement: Release Identity - A deleted identity is released for reuse`
